What is Actuarial Science?

Actuarial Science is the discipline that applies mathematical and statistical methods to assess risk in insurance, finance, and other industries. Actuaries are the professionals who price insurance policies, design pension schemes, and manage financial uncertainty.

At the University of Cape Coast, the program equips students with a strong foundation in probability, statistics, financial mathematics, economics, and computing — the exact tools used by actuaries worldwide.
