Common Questions

How do I contact a lecturer?

Open their profile and use the email or phone button. In-platform messaging is being built for Stage 2.

Can I request a study session with a mentor?

Yes. Send a message describing what you need — many mentors offer one-on-one sessions.

Who can become a mentor?

Senior students, alumni, and lecturers. Executives will reach out when nominations open.

Is there a cost to use the Learning Hub?

No. The Learning Hub is free for all registered Actuarial Science students at UCC.
