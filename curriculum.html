The Curriculum

A four-year journey through mathematics, statistics, economics, and computing. Semester by semester — from Level 100 to Level 400.

How the Program Is Structured

UCC operates on a semester system. Each level builds on the previous one — from broad mathematical foundations to specialist actuarial topics.

📐

Foundation First

Level 100 builds your mathematical and statistical base — algebra, calculus, probability, and computing.

📊

Build & Apply

Level 200 extends into further calculus, abstract algebra, deeper probability, accounting, and quantitative methods.

🎯

Specialise

Levels 300 and 400 dive into advanced actuarial modelling, life contingencies, and research.

Level 100 · Semester 1

Foundation Year — First Semester

Your entry into the language of mathematics and computing. This semester builds the essential toolkit for everything that follows.

MAT 101

Algebra and Trigonometry

Sets, functions, equations, trigonometric identities, and the algebraic foundations for later calculus.

ECO 103

Principles of Microeconomics

Supply and demand, consumer behaviour, market structures, and the economic reasoning you'll apply to actuarial problems.

STA 101

Introduction to Probability

Basic probability, random variables, distributions, and an introduction to statistical thinking.

CSC 105

Introduction to Computing

Computer fundamentals, spreadsheets, problem-solving with computers, and an introduction to programming concepts.

Level 100 · Semester 2

Foundation Year — Second Semester

Continuing the foundation. Courses for this semester will be added as the department publishes them.

Level 200

Building the Core

Year two deepens your mathematical maturity — further calculus, abstract algebra, advanced probability, accounting, and quantitative methods.

MAT 203

Further Calculus

Multivariable calculus, partial derivatives, multiple integrals, and series — the calculus toolkit for actuarial modelling.

MAT 201

Abstract Algebra

Groups, rings, and fields. A rigorous introduction to algebraic structures and proof techniques.

STA 203

Further Probability

Joint distributions, transformations, moment-generating functions, and deeper probability theory.

ECO 207

Elements of Microeconomics

Intermediate microeconomic theory — consumer choice, production, market equilibrium, and welfare.

ACC 101

Foundations of Accounting

Principles of financial accounting — the language of business and the foundation for actuarial finance.

SBU 201

Quantitative Methods

Mathematical and statistical techniques applied to business decision-making and operations.

Level 300

Actuarial Core

The third year moves into specialist actuarial territory — life contingencies, financial mathematics, and advanced statistical modelling.

Where This Takes You

Actuarial Science graduates from UCC pursue careers in many directions.

🛡

Insurance & Pensions

Actuary, underwriter, pension analyst, claims specialist.

🏦

Banking & Finance

Risk analyst, investment officer, credit modeller, quantitative analyst.

📊

Data & Analytics

Data scientist, statistician, business analyst, machine-learning engineer.

📚

Academia

Lecturer, researcher, or postgraduate study in actuarial science or statistics.

🌍

International Exams

Pursue IFoA (UK) or SOA (US) professional qualifications alongside work.

🚀

Entrepreneurship

Fintech, insurtech, and consulting — many actuary-led startups exist.
