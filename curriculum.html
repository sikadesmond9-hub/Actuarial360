The Curriculum

A four-year journey through mathematics, statistics, finance, and computing. Here's what you'll study — level by level.

How the Program Is Structured

UCC operates on a semester system. Each level (100–400) builds on the previous one, moving from broad foundations to specialist actuarial topics.

📐

Foundation First

Level 100 focuses on core mathematics and statistics — the bedrock of everything.

📊

Build & Apply

Levels 200–300 apply probability, calculus, and finance to real problems.

🎯

Specialise

Level 400 dives into advanced actuarial modelling, life contingencies, and research.

Level 100

Foundation Year

The first year grounds you in the language of mathematics. You'll strengthen algebra, calculus, and statistics, while also taking university-wide courses.

MAT 101

Algebra & Trigonometry

Sets, functions, equations, trigonometric identities — the toolkit for later courses.

MAT 102

Calculus I

Limits, differentiation, and integration of single-variable functions.

STA 101

Introduction to Statistics

Descriptive statistics, probability basics, and data interpretation.

STA 102

Probability Theory I

Random variables, distributions, expectation, and variance.

ECO 101

Principles of Economics

Micro and macro foundations — essential context for actuarial work.

CSC 101

Introduction to Computing

Computer fundamentals, spreadsheets, and an introduction to programming.

Level 200

Building the Core

Year two applies the foundations. You'll take multivariable calculus, deeper probability, and your first financial mathematics course.

MAT 201

Calculus II

Multivariable calculus, partial derivatives, and multiple integrals.

MAT 202

Linear Algebra

Matrices, vector spaces, eigenvalues — crucial for modelling.

STA 201

Probability Theory II

Joint distributions, transformations, moment-generating functions.

STA 202

Statistical Inference

Estimation, hypothesis testing, and confidence intervals.

ACT 201

Financial Mathematics I

Time value of money, interest rates, annuities, and present values.

ECO 201

Macroeconomics

National income, inflation, monetary policy, and economic growth.

Level 300

Actuarial Core

The third year is where the actuarial identity takes shape. Life contingencies, financial economics, and advanced statistics.

ACT 301

Life Contingencies I

Survival models, life tables, and pricing of life insurance products.

ACT 302

Financial Mathematics II

Bonds, term structure of interest rates, and investment returns.

ACT 303

Actuarial Modelling

Building and applying models for insurance and financial risks.

STA 301

Regression Analysis

Linear and multiple regression — the foundation of predictive analytics.

STA 302

Stochastic Processes

Markov chains, Poisson processes, and random walks.

ACT 304

Economics for Actuaries

Financial markets, asset pricing, and risk/return relationships.

Level 400

Advanced & Specialisation

The final year. Advanced actuarial science, research methods, and your final project. You'll emerge ready for the profession or postgraduate study.

ACT 401

Life Contingencies II

Multi-life models, pension mathematics, and reserves.

ACT 402

Risk Theory

Ruin theory, collective risk models, and reinsurance.

ACT 403

Pensions & Social Insurance

Design and valuation of pension schemes and social security systems.

ACT 404

Actuarial Statistics

Credibility theory, loss distributions, and general insurance models.

ACT 405

Financial Economics

Portfolio theory, derivatives, and actuarial applications in finance.

ACT 499

Final Year Project

An independent research project applying actuarial methods to a real problem.

Where This Takes You

Actuarial Science graduates from UCC pursue careers in many directions.

🛡

Insurance & Pensions

Actuary, underwriter, pension analyst, claims specialist.

🏦

Banking & Finance

Risk analyst, investment officer, credit modeller, quantitative analyst.

📊

Data & Analytics

Data scientist, statistician, business analyst, machine-learning engineer.

📚

Academia

Lecturer, researcher, or postgraduate study in actuarial science or statistics.

🌍

International Exams

Pursue IFoA (UK) or SOA (US) professional qualifications alongside work.

🚀

Entrepreneurship

Fintech, insurtech, and consulting — many actuary-led startups exist.
