What You'll Study

A four-year journey through mathematics, statistics, finance and computing — building the foundation every actuary needs.

Program Overview

Actuarial Science at UCC is a 4-year Bachelor's program combining core mathematics with applied actuarial, financial and statistical courses.

Level 100 — Foundations

Building the mathematical base every actuary needs.

MAT 101

Calculus I

Limits, derivatives, integrals and their applications.

MAT 102

Calculus II

Sequences, series, multivariable calculus.

MAT 103

Algebra & Trigonometry

Sets, functions, polynomials, basic trig identities.

STA 101

Introduction to Statistics

Descriptive statistics, probability basics, distributions.

CSC 101

Introduction to Computing

Computer fundamentals, algorithms, and problem solving.

ECO 101

Principles of Economics

Micro and macro foundations — supply, demand, markets.

UGRC

University Required Courses

Communication skills, African studies, and liberal electives.

Level 200 — Probability & Statistics

Deeper into probability theory, inference and mathematical rigor.

MAT 201

Linear Algebra

Vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, linear transformations.

MAT 202

Real Analysis

Sequences, continuity, limits and rigorous proof.

STA 201

Probability Theory

Random variables, distributions, expectation, variance.

STA 202

Statistical Inference

Estimation, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals.

ACT 201

Introduction to Actuarial Science

Role of the actuary, insurance basics, risk concepts.

CSC 201

Programming (Python/R)

Data analysis and actuarial computations with code.

ECO 201

Macroeconomics

National income, inflation, monetary policy.

Level 300 — Actuarial Core

The heart of the program: financial mathematics, life contingencies and risk.

ACT 301

Financial Mathematics

Interest theory, annuities, bonds, NPV and cash flows.

ACT 302

Life Contingencies I

Life tables, survival models, life insurance and annuities.

ACT 303

Risk Theory

Risk models, ruin theory, credibility.

STA 301

Stochastic Processes

Markov chains, Poisson processes, applications.

STA 302

Regression & Modelling

Linear models, GLMs, practical data modelling.

ECO 301

Financial Economics

Asset pricing, portfolio theory, market efficiency.

Level 400 — Advanced & Specialised

Professional-level topics and a final-year research project.

ACT 401

Life Contingencies II

Advanced life insurance models, reserves, profit testing.

ACT 402

Pension Mathematics

Design, funding and valuation of pension schemes.

ACT 403

Actuarial Modelling

Simulation, Monte Carlo, model building and validation.

ACT 404

General Insurance

Pricing and reserving for non-life insurance.

ACT 405

Project / Dissertation

Supervised research on a real actuarial problem.

STA 401

Advanced Statistical Methods

Time series, Bayesian methods, statistical learning.

ⓘ Course codes and titles shown here reflect the general structure of a UCC Actuarial Science degree. Always confirm the current official course list with the Department of Mathematics.

Where This Takes You

Career paths open to Actuarial Science graduates.

🛡

Insurance Actuary

Pricing, reserving and risk management at life or general insurers.

🏦

Investment Analyst

Banks, asset managers and pension funds.

📊

Data Scientist

Analytics, machine learning, and financial modelling.

🌍

International Exams

Qualify with IFoA (UK) or SOA (USA) to work globally.
